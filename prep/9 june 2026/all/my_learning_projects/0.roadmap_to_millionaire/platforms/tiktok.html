🎵 TikTok

⚠️ India note: TikTok remains banned in India (since 2020). Our India-lens audience lives on Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts instead. TikTok still matters for the global/US tech audience — the highest-value ad market.

How the Algorithm Works

The For You Page is the purest interest-graph algorithm: follower count is nearly irrelevant, every video is tested independently.

  • Batch testing: new video → shown to a few hundred users → completion rate, rewatches, likes, comments, shares measured → strong performance unlocks the next, larger batch.
  • Completion rate is king — videos of 21–34s show the highest completion; watching to the end (and rewatching) is the strongest signal.
  • Early velocity matters: the first hours decide a video's ceiling.
  • Interest signals: sounds, hashtags, captions, and what similar users watched.
Playbook: instant hook, no intro, 21–34s (but >1 minute for Creator Rewards eligibility — a real tension: use short videos to grow, longer ones to earn), trending sounds, reply-to-comments videos to farm conversation.
