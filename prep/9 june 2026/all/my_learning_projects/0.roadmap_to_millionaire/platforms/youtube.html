How the Algorithm Works

YouTube doesn't have one algorithm — it has ranking systems per surface (Home, Suggested, Search, Shorts feed). All optimize for viewer satisfaction, measured by:

  • Watch time & retention — the dominant signal. For Shorts: "viewed vs swiped away" ratio.
  • CTR — thumbnail/title effectiveness (long-form; Shorts don't use thumbnails in feed).
  • Engagement — likes, comments, shares, subscriptions gained from the video.
  • Satisfaction surveys & history — YouTube directly asks viewers and personalizes accordingly.

Shorts specifics: each Short is tested on a small batch of viewers; strong swipe-stay rates and completion/replay unlock exponentially larger batches. Subscriber count barely matters for Shorts distribution — a new channel can go viral on video #1.

Playbook: hook in ≤2s, 30–45s length for high completion, loopable endings encourage replays, consistent niche trains the audience-match. Search-friendly titles ("Why [AI tool] is blowing up") compound over time.
