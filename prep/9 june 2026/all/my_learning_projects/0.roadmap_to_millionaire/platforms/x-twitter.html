  • Conversation wins: posts that trigger replies (and where the author replies back) get compounding reach.
  • External links are deprioritized in-feed — put links in a reply, not the main post.
  • Premium subscribers get a ranking boost; media (images/video) outperforms plain text.
Playbook: threads breaking down "what's blowing up in AI today and why", hot-take framing that invites replies, always reply to early commenters, bookmark-worthy list posts (our Top-100 excerpts are natural bookmark bait).
