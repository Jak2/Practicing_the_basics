How the Algorithm Works (2026)

Separate ranking per surface (Feed, Reels, Stories, Explore). Head of Instagram Adam Mosseri confirmed the top-3 signals:

  • Watch time — especially watch-through and replay rate on Reels.
  • Sends per reach — how often people DM your post to friends. The single most weighted Reels distribution signal.
  • Likes per reach — quality vote normalized by audience size.

2026 shifts: shares, watch-through rate, saves, replay rate lead (roughly in that order); Instagram is prioritizing "raw, real human content" over obviously AI-generated material; original content is favored over reposts (reposted Reels get suppressed and credited to the original).

Playbook: make Reels people want to send to a friend ("did you see this AI thing?!" is inherently sendable). Use trending audio, native text overlays, and post consistently. India is Instagram's largest market — our India-lens content has home-field advantage here.

Reality check: Instagram has no open, reliable ad-revenue share like YouTube. Direct platform payouts are weak/invite-based. Money on Instagram = brand deals, affiliates, and funneling to owned products.

Reward Policy Notes

  • Branded content requires the Paid Partnership label; FTC disclosure rules apply.
  • Originality policy: aggregator/repost accounts get reduced distribution — commentary transformation matters here too.
  • No public per-view rate; bonus programs historically paid wildly variable amounts and come and go.
Our strategy: Instagram is a reach and brand-deal platform, not a payout platform. Cross-post our Shorts as Reels, grow the India audience, use it to sell the trend report and land sponsorships once past 10K.
