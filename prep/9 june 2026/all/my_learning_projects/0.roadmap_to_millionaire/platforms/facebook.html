📘 Facebook

How the Algorithm Works

  • Feed ranking predicts what each user finds valuable: meaningful interactions (comments, shares, reactions — especially between friends) outweigh passive views.
  • Video/Reels ranking mirrors Instagram: watch time, completion, shares.
  • Original content is prioritized; unoriginal/reposted content is suppressed and ineligible for monetization.
  • Huge under-30 audience decline, but massive 30+ and international (incl. India) reach — different demographic than TikTok/IG.

Monetization: Content Monetization Program (2026)

Meta consolidated in-stream ads, Reels bonuses, and performance bonuses into one Content Monetization Program (CMP) — a single performance-based payout across videos, Reels, photos and text posts.

Reward Policy Notes

  • Originality is enforced hard: watermarked or recycled content is ineligible for CMP.
  • Longer videos (1–3+ min) with in-stream ads pay far better than Reels here — the opposite of TikTok's short-form economics.
