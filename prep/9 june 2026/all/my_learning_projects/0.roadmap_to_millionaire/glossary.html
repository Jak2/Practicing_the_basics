📖 Social Media Marketing Glossary

The terms a social media marketing manager uses daily — explained plainly, with examples relevant to our trend-analyst project.

💰 Money Metrics

CPM — Cost Per Mille (per 1,000 impressions)
What an advertiser pays the platform to show an ad 1,000 times. High-CPM niches (tech, AI, finance: $15–$50+) mean advertisers pay more, so creators in those niches earn more.
For us: tech/AI content has one of the highest CPMs on YouTube — a core reason we picked this niche.
RPM — Revenue Per Mille
What the creator actually receives per 1,000 views, after the platform takes its cut (YouTube keeps 45% of long-form ad revenue). RPM is always lower than CPM. Shorts RPM is typically $0.05–$0.10; long-form tech content can be $10–$30.
For us: Shorts build the audience cheaply; long-form videos later harvest the high RPM.
ARR — Annual Recurring Revenue
Total yearly value of active subscriptions for a subscription business. 1,000 subscribers × $20/month = $240K ARR.
For us: Exploding Topics runs $3.2M ARR — the benchmark for our trend-intelligence report product.
MRR — Monthly Recurring Revenue
ARR ÷ 12. The month-to-month heartbeat of a subscription product.
ROAS — Return On Ad Spend
Revenue generated per $1 spent on ads. ROAS of 4 = $4 back per $1 spent. Matters if we ever pay to promote our report.
CAC — Customer Acquisition Cost
Total marketing spend ÷ new customers gained. Our organic content strategy means near-zero CAC for the report product — the content is the marketing.
LTV — Lifetime Value
Total revenue one customer generates before leaving. Healthy subscription businesses aim for LTV ≥ 3× CAC.

📊 Audience & Performance Metrics

Impressions vs Reach
Impressions = total times content was displayed (one person can count many times). Reach = unique people who saw it.
Engagement Rate
(Likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ reach (or followers). 1–3% is average; 5%+ is strong. Algorithms use it as a quality vote.
Watch Time / Retention / Watch-Through Rate
How long viewers stay. The #1 ranking signal on YouTube and Reels. A 30-second Short watched fully (100% retention) beats a 3-minute video abandoned at 20 seconds.
For us: our scripts must hook in the first 2 seconds and stay tight — retention decides distribution.
CTR — Click-Through Rate
Clicks ÷ impressions. On YouTube: thumbnail/title effectiveness. On affiliate links: how many viewers actually tap through.
Sends per Reach
Instagram-specific: how often viewers DM your post to a friend, divided by reach. Confirmed by Instagram's head as one of its top-3 ranking signals — shareable content wins.
Velocity
How fast a post accumulates engagement in its first hour(s). Platforms test content on a small sample; strong early velocity unlocks bigger distribution waves.

🧲 Growth & Content Terms

Hook
The first 1–3 seconds of a video that decide whether the viewer stays. Pattern interrupts, bold claims, curiosity gaps.
Niche
The focused topic territory an account owns. Algorithms build an audience profile per account — consistency trains them to find your people.
UGC — User-Generated Content
Content made by users rather than brands. Also a paid gig: brands pay "UGC creators" for ad-style content without requiring their audience.
Shadowban
Unannounced distribution suppression when a platform suspects spam, reposted content, or guideline issues. Why we transform (commentary) rather than repost.
Evergreen vs Trending Content
Evergreen stays relevant for years (glossaries, how-tos); trending spikes and dies in days. Our engine rides trends; our report archive becomes evergreen.
SEO — Search Engine Optimization
Making content findable via search. YouTube is the world's #2 search engine — titles/descriptions with searched keywords compound over time.

🤝 Monetization Routes

Ad Revenue Share
Platform splits ad money with creators (YouTube Partner Program, TikTok Creator Rewards, X ads share, Facebook Content Monetization). Requires passing eligibility gates.
Affiliate Marketing
Earning a commission for sales via your tracked link. No follower threshold — works from day one. Typical: 5–30% per sale (AI tools often pay 20–30% recurring).
Brand Deal / Sponsorship
A brand pays for placement in your content. Rates roughly $10–$25 per 1,000 followers per post; AI/tech niche and virtual influencers command premiums ($2K–$15K/post at 100K+ followers). FTC requires disclosure (#ad), and AI personas need double disclosure (sponsored + AI).
Fan Funding
Direct viewer payments: Super Thanks, TikTok LIVE gifts, Instagram badges, memberships, Patreon.
Digital Products
Selling your own goods — courses, templates, and in our case the Top-100 trend report subscription. Highest margin, fully owned.
