# Health Module — Fit, Gut, Physique, Agility, Stamina, Mental Peace

*Added 2026-07-03. Source material: `../health/` (3 files, reviewed + cross-checked online by Claude). This module upgrades the daily spine's "physical act" — it does NOT add new tracked habits. The streak still counts only the 3-item spine.*

## Verdict on the `../health/` files (read this before trusting them)

- **Gut health schedule + shopping list: good raw material, too heavy to follow.** The foods and Ayurvedic staples (psyllium, triphala, jeera/fennel/ajwain teas, curd/buttermilk probiotics, seed rotations, budget swaps) are sound and cheap — daily psyllium is well-evidenced as safe, triphala is likely safe but start low (mild GI upset early is common). **But** the precise stats in the file ("32% enzyme improvement", "25% less gas") are unverifiable AI-generated numbers — ignore them. And a 12-touchpoint timed daily schedule is exactly the "start strong, drop off" trap in `me.md`. Distilled to 5 anchors below.
- **Weight-loss file: right ideas, wrong persona and unsafe promise.** It's written for an outdoor sun-exposed laborer who naps midday and drinks — not an AI engineer at a desk. Its core moves (protein-forward Indian meals, buffer meals, no-hunger approach) are solid. Its promise of **3–5 kg/month and 15–25 kg in 6 months is above the evidence-based safe rate: 0.5–1 kg/week (≈2–4 kg/month)**; faster risks muscle loss and rebound. Target **2–3 kg/month max**.
- **What all three files miss entirely:** strength training, flexibility/agility work, endurance progression, and mental peace. They are diet-only. Filled in below.

## The one rule change

The daily spine's **"physical act — 10 min of anything"** stays the floor. This module defines what a *good* physical act looks like when you have more than 10 minutes. A bad day = 10-min walk = full success, unchanged.

## Physical menu (rotate; ~30–45 min when time allows)

Evidence: even 1×/week resistance work improves strength, body comp, and flexibility; ~3 short sessions/week captures most gains. No gym required.

| Day type | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Strength (2×/week)** | Bodyweight circuit at home: squats, push-ups (knee if needed), lunges, plank, glute bridge — 3 rounds, 8–12 reps each. Progress by adding reps, then a backpack with books. |
| **Stamina (2×/week)** | Brisk 30–40 min walk (podcast/hands-free interview-prep audio counts as job-search adjacent). Once a week make it intervals: 1 min fast / 2 min easy × 8. |
| **Agility + flexibility (2×/week)** | 15 min: sun salutations ×5, deep squat hold, hip openers, shoulder circles, hamstring stretch. Any yoga flow video works. |
| **Rest (1×/week)** | Gentle stroll or nothing. Rest is part of the program. |

Order doesn't matter. Missed days aren't made up — just do the next one.

**Progression (so this doesn't go stale):**
- **Weeks 1–2 (on-ramp):** half of everything — 2 rounds not 3, 20-min walks, no intervals. Surviving the on-ramp matters more than the numbers.
- **Then:** each week add ~1–2 reps per exercise OR 5 min to a walk — one small increase, not all. When 3×12 push-ups is easy, elevate feet or add the backpack. When 40-min walks are easy, add a second interval day.
- **Monthly:** re-measure the baseline block above. The waist number and push-up count are the progress report, not the mirror.

## Food anchors (5, not 12 — distilled from `../health/`)

1. **Wake:** warm water + lemon; 2–3×/week add soaked chia/methi seeds. (Psyllium ½ tsp is fine if constipated; triphala only if you want it, start ¼ tsp.)
2. **Every lunch:** one real salad (cucumber/carrot/onion/tomato/sprouts/chana — any combo) + dal/rajma/chana for protein. Millet roti or brown rice over white when easy.
3. **Daily probiotic:** homemade curd or buttermilk with jeera/mint. ₹10/day beats ₹500/month supplements — this claim in the file is legit.
4. **Dinner light and done by ~8:30 PM.** Cheela/paneer-veg stir fry/khichdi + salad. Golden milk or herbal tea after if you like it.
5. **Buffer rule (from the weight-loss file — keep this, it's the smartest thing in it):** 1–2 outside/street-food meals per week, guilt-free, choose grilled/boiled over deep-fried when possible. Alcohol if at all: rare, never empty-stomach, water alongside.

Everything else in the gut-health file (seed elixir rotations, CCF teas, skin scrubs) is **optional garnish** — do it if you enjoy it, never track it, never feel behind for skipping it.

## Mental peace

No new habit. Two existing hooks carry it:
- The **shutdown note** already is the mental-hygiene practice — honest logging closes the day's loop.
- The **stamina walks** double as it: phone on do-not-disturb, outdoors if possible. If you want more: 5 min of slow nasal breathing (pranayama) before sleep — optional, untracked.

## Expectations (honest version, replacing the files' hype)

- Weeks 1–2: less bloating, better sleep — plausible.
- Month 1–3: 2–3 kg/month fat loss if food anchors hold, visible stamina gain from walks, easier deep-squat.
- Sunlight 15–20 min/morning genuinely covers most vitamin D needs — the file is right. B12: get tested once (common deficiency in vegetarian Indian diets); supplement only per doctor.
- Any sharp pain, blood, or persistent gut symptoms → doctor, not this file.

## Weekly review hook

The Sunday review may ask ONE health question: "did the physical acts happen and how did food feel?" — pattern analysis only, no shame, no expansion of this module without a written reason.
