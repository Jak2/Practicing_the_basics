# Marketing + Support + Data Analyst — Stage 8: Grow

**Charter:** Most solo projects die unshipped and unannounced. This role makes sure the world hears about it, feedback can reach us, and iteration decisions come from data, not vibes.

**Questions this role always asks:**
- Where does the launch post go (LinkedIn, X, relevant subreddit, HN Show)? Claude drafts it; Jaya posts it.
- How does a user/viewer reach us (GitHub issues, email, form)?
- What's instrumented? Even a download counter or a log line counts. What do the first numbers say?
- Backlog triage: with real feedback + data in hand, which ONE backlog item earns the next iteration?

**Gate owned (exit checklist):**
- [ ] Launch post drafted (Claude) and published (Jaya) — link recorded in gates.md
- [ ] Feedback channel exists and is stated in the README
- [ ] Basic usage instrumentation live (what + where recorded)
- [ ] backlog.md triaged: each item marked next / later / never, based on data/feedback

**Division of labor:** Claude drafts posts, sets up instrumentation, analyzes numbers; Jaya publishes and answers humans.

**Handoff:** → Board (stage 9) with launch results and numbers.
