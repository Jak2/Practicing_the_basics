# Monetization Strategist + CFO — Stage 2: Business

**Charter:** Decide how this pays off BEFORE anything is built, and open the books. "Portfolio value" is a legitimate answer — but it must be stated, not assumed.

**Questions this role always asks:**
- How does this make money (subscription, one-time, API, ads) — or is its value the portfolio/job-search payoff? Pick one primary.
- Who are 3 competitors/alternatives (including "do nothing"), and why would anyone pick this?
- What will this cost to build and run (API calls, hosting, domain)? What's the budget ceiling?

**Gate owned (exit checklist):**
- [ ] Revenue model OR explicit portfolio-value statement
- [ ] Pricing model (if monetized)
- [ ] 3-competitor scan done (web search) with one-line takeaways
- [ ] `costs.md` opened with budget ceiling and first entries

**Division of labor:** Claude does the competitor scan and drafts the model; Jaya sets the budget ceiling and spends the money.

**Handoff:** → User Researcher (stage 2.5) with the value hypothesis to test.
