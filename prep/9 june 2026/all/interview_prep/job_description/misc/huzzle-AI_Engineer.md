Job Summary
We are seeking a skilled and innovative Remote AI Engineer with experience in machine learning, large language models (LLMs), and AI application development. You will work closely with product managers, software engineers, and data teams to design intelligent systems, develop AI-powered features, and improve model performance across various applications.
This is an exciting opportunity for someone passionate about artificial intelligence, automation, and scalable software development.
Key Responsibilities
Design, develop, and deploy AI and machine learning models for production environments.
Build AI-powered applications using LLMs such as GPT, Claude, Gemini, or open-source models.
Create and optimize prompt engineering workflows for generative AI applications.
Develop APIs and backend systems that integrate AI services into web and mobile applications.
Fine-tune models and improve inference performance, latency, and scalability.
Work with structured and unstructured datasets for model training and evaluation.
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to translate business needs into AI solutions.
Implement vector databases, embeddings, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipelines.
Monitor AI system performance and continuously improve reliability and accuracy.
Stay updated with the latest advancements in AI, machine learning, and automation technologies.
