## How to actually learn (this matters more than the content)
1. **Read the answer once for understanding.**
2. **Close the file. Say it out loud in your own words.** If you can't, you don't know it yet — reread.
3. **Speak, don't recite.** The goal is to sound like you, thinking — not a recording.
4. **Out loud, always.** Reading silently feels like learning but isn't. Your mouth needs the reps.
5. **If stuck in the real interview:** say *"Let me think through that,"* reason aloud, and if you truly don't know: *"I haven't worked with that directly, but based on [related thing], I'd approach it by…"* Never freeze. Never invent a fake fact.
