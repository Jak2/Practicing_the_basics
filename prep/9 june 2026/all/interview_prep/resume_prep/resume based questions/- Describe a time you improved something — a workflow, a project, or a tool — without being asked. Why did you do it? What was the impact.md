`built Robot framework generator tool to free new joiners to focus on functionality, tests and ease up the learning curve`

Context
	- I was part of a team where there were new joiners and they were having difficult in understanding the basics of robot framework. 
	- They were spending more time in understanding the basics of robot framework and less time in writing tests.
	- The team was also facing issue with maintaining the test cases.
	- So I thought of building a tool to ease up the learning curve and also to maintain the test cases.
